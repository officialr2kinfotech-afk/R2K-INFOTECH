About Us - REKAVO

Welcome to REKAVO - Your Trusted Online Shopping Destination.

At REKAVO, we are building India's most affordable and trusted multi-vendor e-commerce platform where sellers and buyers connect directly.

Who We Are:

REKAVO is an Indian e-commerce marketplace founded in 2026 and based in Sarangpur, Madhya Pradesh. Our platform allows verified sellers from across India to list and sell their products to customers pan-India.

What We Do:

We provide a seamless online shopping experience for customers looking for quality products at best prices in categories like Fashion, Electronics, Home & Kitchen, and more. For sellers, we provide a powerful seller panel (seller.rekavo.in) to manage products, orders, and payments easily.

Our Mission:

Our mission is to empower small and medium businesses of India to go digital and sell online without heavy commissions, and to provide customers with genuine products and fast delivery.

Why Choose REKAVO?

  • 100% Genuine Products from Verified Sellers
  • Secure Payments
  • Fast Delivery Across India
  • Easy Returns & Customer Support
